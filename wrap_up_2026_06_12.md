## 4. What Worked / What Didn't

**One trade carried the entire experiment.**
APP hit its price target on May 28 for +$4,254 (+28.3%). Remove that single trade and the system's profit factor collapses to 0.08 — i.e., it lost money on every other closed position combined. The win rate (44%) and profit factor (1.077) look passable only because APP exists.

**ATR stops worked mechanically but the underlying setups failed quickly.**
Four of five losing trades were stop-hits (ADSK −3.7%, UBER −6.0%, ZBRA −7.4%, MSFT −5.2%). The stops did their job — losses were capped — but none of these names moved in the thesis direction at all. The two quickest (ZBRA in 1.2 days, MSFT in 2.0 days after the June restart) suggest basing patterns were identified but not yet confirmed when the entry was placed; price continued falling through the stop immediately.

**The gross deployment rules were violated materially during weeks 2–3.**
By May 13, the book was running at 138.7% gross margin (−$38,966 cash on $97,992 equity), directly breaching the 95% cap that was supposed to be in place. The cap wasn't wired into the live execution path until May 18. This produced a book that couldn't generate learning (execute.yml blocked all new buys while gross was above cap) and carried real blow-up risk on a margin call.

**Two clean-slate restarts diluted what was learned.**
The experiment effectively restarted from cash twice — May 27 (flattened due to margin) and June 8 (voluntary restart to rebuild under corrected rules). The "final" strategy design (post-June 6: honest objective, regime gate, observability ledger, reconciled stats) ran for only 4 trading days before the experiment ended. There is no basis to evaluate that version yet.

## 5. Recommendation

**Continue paper trading another 6 weeks** — but the original +1%/week target has already been correctly retired (June 6 commit) in favor of "preserve capital and beat a passive SPY+cash blend risk-adjusted." Do not reopen the old target.

The case for continuing: the infrastructure is now meaningfully better (fill ledger, reconciled stats, regime gate, honest AM prompts), and the current 9-trade sample is too small to draw conclusions about a system that changed fundamentally three times. The case against: the backtest across 2019–2025 showed 0/5 gates passed and consistent underperformance vs buy-and-hold SPY, so any new 6-week stretch is still a learning exercise, not evidence of alpha. The realistic decision rule is: **if the next 6 weeks produce a profit factor > 1.5 on 15+ closed trades with no gross-cap violations, then consider live deployment at minimum size; otherwise stop and hold SPY passively.**
